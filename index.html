This is a living record rather than a finished answer. I have left spaces for my own photograph, responses and annotation pages so that the site can grow with my work.

02 / The journey

A long scroll through textuality

Every medium
leaves a trace.

Move through the timeline to see how texts have been created, preserved, shared and experienced.

01
◌

ORAL

Before the page, there was the voice.

Knowledge travelled through memory, performance and listening. Each telling could carry a small change, making the community part of the text’s survival.

02
⌁

WRITTEN

Words found a surface to stay on.

Writing gave language a durable form. Texts could travel across time and distance, though access to reading and writing remained uneven.

03
▤

PRINT

Reproduction changed the scale of reading.

Print made identical copies possible and helped texts circulate more widely. The page became a familiar technology for organising knowledge.

04
▥

MASS MEDIA

Texts began to arrive everywhere at once.

Newspapers, radio, cinema and television connected text with image, sound and public life. The audience became larger—and less visible to the maker.

05
▦

DIGITAL

The text became a moving interface.

Digital texts can be searched, linked, remixed and read across screens. Meaning now emerges through networks, design, code and the reader’s choices.
